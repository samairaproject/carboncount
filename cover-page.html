🌍 Why this matters

Transportation is one of the largest sources of household carbon emissions, but it's also one of the most personal — shaped by where you live, what your body needs, and what infrastructure is actually available to you. CarbonCount treats accessibility and climate impact as one connected problem, not two separate conversations. Read more about the problem →

Travel Mode Comparison

A quick look at how common travel modes compare on carbon impact and accessibility.

Travel Mode Est. CO₂ per Mile Typically Accessible?
Driving a car ~0.89 lbs Yes
Public transit (bus/train) ~0.45 lbs Varies by station
Accessible routing (wheelchair/powerchair) ~0.10 lbs Depends on ramps/elevators
Walking or biking ~0.00 lbs Not always (distance, terrain)
